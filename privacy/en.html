Privacy Policy

App: TAKUMIKI ENBU 1.0
Operator: Takumikienbu
Last updated: October 14, 2026

Data collection

The app does not collect data. It has no account, sign-in, ads, tracking, or analytics SDK. The game works offline and does not send information to a developer server.

On-device storage

Game progress, machines obtained, battle records, medals, story history, and language, sound, controls and display settings are stored on your device so you can continue playing. The developer does not receive this information. The app has no custom cloud synchronization. The operating system may include app data in device backups.

Networking and external links

Version 1.0 does not use online play or Game Center. Models, images, fonts, music, sound effects and voices are bundled with the app. Opening the Privacy Policy link in Settings displays this page in Safari. The browser and hosting service policies apply when visiting external websites.

Support requests

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any images or device information you choose to send. We use this information to reply and investigate the issue, keep it for the period needed to provide support, and delete it when no longer needed. You may request deletion at the address below.

Deleting saved data

Deleting the app from your device deletes its local app data. Manage operating system backups through the relevant service settings. The developer cannot remotely read or delete data on your device.
