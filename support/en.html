TAKUMIKI ENBU Support

TAKUMIKI ENBU 1.0 includes Chapter One, the Spring Basho. It runs on iPhone with iOS 16.0 or later, works offline, and needs no sign-in. There are no ads or in-app purchases.

Tell us the app version, iPhone model, iOS version, and steps that cause the issue. You may include screenshots without personal information.

No sound

Check Silent Mode and the device volume. Open Settings at the bottom of the home screen to adjust music, sound effects, and voice volume. Story voices are Japanese; subtitles follow the selected language.

Controls and language

You can use touch controls or a compatible game controller. Portrait and landscape are supported. Practice on the home screen lets you try the controls. Change the language in Settings.

Saved progress

Progress is saved on this iPhone. There is no automatic synchronization to another device. Deleting the app also deletes local progress.
